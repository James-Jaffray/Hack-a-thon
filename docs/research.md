# Research notes

Hack-a-thon, November 2026. These are the team's raw notes from talking to people before we built CalHub. The summary lives in the [README](../README.md).

## Do people use calendars?

- Corporate and working people: the majority say yes.
- Students: the majority say no.
  - Why? People forget, and no habit is formed.

## Which platform do they use?

- Mainly Android (for software developers).
- Lots of people use both: Android on laptops and desktops, then Apple phones.
- One person uses a physical calendar.

## How many times is it checked in a day?

- Between 0 and 10 times.
  - Average is 2 to 4 times.
  - It depends on whether they know they have a busy week.

## Notifications

- People prefer to turn notifications on, but do not want to be overwhelmed.
  - They like to be notified 30 minutes to 1 hour before an event starts.

## Questions we asked ourselves

- Who is our target audience?
- If people are happy with their Google calendars, why would they switch to us?

## Pain points with people's current calendar experience (ideas to implement)

- Export and import: not easy to do for calendars when integrating them into a different app.
- Labels: maybe use AI to automatically tag or categorize the event you input (create a toggle).
- When entering an event, the app automatically uses the same time as your last similar event, and people find that annoying (create a toggle).
- It is hard to find dates sometimes.
- Checking different devices is annoying because they do not always sync.
